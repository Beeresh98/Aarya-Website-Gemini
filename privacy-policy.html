Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information Aarya Plastopet (“we”, “us”) collects when you use www.aaryaplastopet.com, contact us through its forms, or message us on WhatsApp, and how we use and protect it.

Who we are

Aarya Plastopet, Machenahalli Industrial Area, 1st Cross, Bhadravathi, Shimoga, Karnataka, India. Email: info@aaryaplastopet.com. Phone / WhatsApp: +91 74836 43028.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in an enquiry or quote form: name, company or farm, email, phone or WhatsApp number, town, city or country, volume and your message.
  • Messages you send us by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Usage information collected automatically through Google Analytics: pages visited, approximate location (city level), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. This uses cookies.
  • Server logs kept by our web host, such as IP address and time of visit, used for security and to keep the site running.

We do not ask for payment details, identity documents or passwords on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, send prices and quotes, and supply your order.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp, phone or email about the film you asked about.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the website.
  • To keep business, tax and invoice records as the law requires.

We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing messages unless you have asked us to keep in touch.

Services that handle your information for us

  • EmailJS delivers enquiry forms to our email inbox.
  • Google Firebase stores quote requests sent from our REINLAND 22 µ page in our private database.
  • Google Analytics measures website use. Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries the chats you start with us.
  • Hostinger hosts this website. Fonts come from Google Fonts and one script from jsDelivr.

Some of these providers may process information on servers outside India. They only receive what is needed to provide their service.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and messages are kept for as long as we need them to answer you and to keep our business records, and are then deleted. Google Analytics data is kept for the period set in our Analytics account.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) to count visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google’s opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. The site works without them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, for visitors in the European Union or United Kingdom, the GDPR, you may also withdraw consent and complain to the data protection authority where you live. Write to info@aaryaplastopet.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS, and access to enquiries is limited to our own staff.

Children

This website is for businesses and farmers. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
