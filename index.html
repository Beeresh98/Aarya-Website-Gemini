Our Stretch Film Solutions

Tailored for every industrial application, our films guarantee optimal performance and cost-efficiency.

Machine Grade Stretch Film

Machine Grade Stretch Film

Designed for high-speed, automated pallet wrapping. Our machine films offer excellent pre-stretch capabilities (up to 350%), reducing material consumption while ensuring maximum load stability.

  • High Tensile Strength & Puncture Resistance
  • Consistent Thickness and Cling
  • Ideal for High-Volume Logistics & Warehousing
  • Available in Various Gauges and Widths

Hand Grade Stretch Film

Hand Grade Stretch Film

Our manual wrap films are lightweight, easy to handle, and provide exceptional cling and strength. Perfect for lower volume operations, bundling, or securing irregular loads without machinery.

  • Superior Clarity for Barcode Scanning
  • Excellent Load Retention
  • Ergonomic and Easy to Apply
  • Cost-Effective for Manual Packaging Needs

Silage Stretch Film

Silage Stretch Film

Available in various colors (opaque or tinted) for easy load identification, inventory management, or adding a layer of security by concealing contents.

  • Quick Product Categorization
  • Tamper-Evident Security
  • Company Branding Opportunity
  • Reduces Risk of Pilferage

UV Resistant Film

UV Resistant Film

Specially formulated with UV inhibitors to protect products from sun degradation during outdoor storage or transit, extending product shelf life.

  • Up to 12 Months UV Protection
  • Prevents Discoloration and Damage
  • Ideal for Agricultural & Construction Goods
  • Maintains Film Integrity Outdoors

Pre-Stretched Film

Pre-Stretched Film

Stretched close to its ultimate breaking point before being wound onto rolls. This results in a lighter, more efficient roll that requires less user effort to apply.

  • Up to 50% Film Savings
  • Easier and Faster Application
  • Reduced Physical Strain on Workers
  • Excellent Load Containment

Vented Pallet Wrap

Vented Pallet Wrap

Engineered with die-cut holes to allow airflow. Ideal for products that need to breathe, such as fresh produce, frozen foods, or items that are hot-filled.

  • Prevents Condensation and Spoilage
  • Faster Cooling or Freezing
  • Maintains Load Integrity
  • Perfect for Food & Beverage Industry
