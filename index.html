Our factory

From pellet to pallet, under one roof.

Every roll is made, checked and packed at the Machenahalli Production Unit in Shimoga, Karnataka.

01

Virgin LLDPE

We start from fresh LLDPE resin. No reprocessed blends.

02

Cast on our line

The film is cast, cooled and wound on our own extrusion line.

03

Checked roll by roll

Batches are tested for stretch, strength and puncture before packing.

04

Boxed and palletised

Rolls go into cartons, cartons onto pallets, pallets get wrapped.

05

Shipped

Trucks across South India. Containers to ports for export.
