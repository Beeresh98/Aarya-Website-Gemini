About Aarya Plastopet

Makers of stretch film and REINLAND silage wrap.

Aarya Plastopet is a plastic film manufacturer at the Machenahalli Production Unit in Bhadravathi, Shimoga district, Karnataka. We make LLDPE stretch film for packaging and REINLAND silage wrap for farms, and supply them across India and to buyers abroad.

Our brands

Two films, one name behind them.

Everything we sell is made at Machenahalli. Stretch film goes out under the Aarya Plastopet name; silage wrap goes out as REINLAND, in the carton with the tractor on it.

Aarya Plastopet stretch film

Hand and machine grade LLDPE pallet wrap for factories, warehouses and distributors. India · Export

REINLAND silage wrap

UV-stabilised bale wrap for dairy farms and bale makers: REINLAND-60 for everyday use, REINLAND-400 for long storage. India · Export

Private label for export

Our factory

From pellet to pallet, under one roof.

Every roll is made, checked and packed at the Machenahalli Production Unit, Bhadravathi, Shimoga, Karnataka.

01

Virgin LLDPE

We start from fresh LLDPE resin. No reprocessed blends.

02

Made on our line

Film is cast or blown, cooled and wound at our own plant.

03

Checked roll by roll

Batches are tested for stretch, strength and puncture before packing.

04

Boxed and palletised

Rolls go into cartons, cartons onto pallets, pallets get wrapped.

05

Shipped

Trucks across India. Containers to ports for export.
