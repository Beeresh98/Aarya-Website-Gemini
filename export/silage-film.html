Questions

Asked by buyers like you.

Can you supply REINLAND under our own brand?
Yes. Private label and OEM options are available on export orders.
What widths and thickness do you export?
500 mm and 750 mm widths as standard, with thickness, colour (white, green or black) and roll length set to your market.
Do you ship full container loads?
Yes. We supply full container loads (FCL) to distributors and large agricultural co-operatives, palletised and labelled.
How long is the UV protection?
10–12 months on REINLAND-60 and 18 months on REINLAND-400.
Do you quote FOB or CIF?
Both. Send your destination port and volume and we will quote.
Why is there no price on the website?
Film prices depend on width, thickness, roll length, quantity and delivery location, so we quote every order. Send your requirement on the form or on WhatsApp and we will reply with a price.
