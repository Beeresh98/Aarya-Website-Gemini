Questions

Asked by buyers like you.

Do you quote FOB or CIF?
Both. Tell us your destination port and volume and we will quote FOB or CIF.
What thickness and sizes can you supply for export?
Standard hand and machine grades from 300 to 600 mm wide, plus custom thickness from 12 to 50 µm. Core size and roll length can be matched to your market.
How is the film packed for shipping?
Rolls go into strong outer cartons, cartons are stacked on pallets and stretch-wrapped, and loads are planned to fill 20 ft or 40 ft containers.
Do you handle export documents?
Yes. We label each carton and prepare the documents needed for export.
Where is the stretch film made?
At the Machenahalli Production Unit in Bhadravathi, Shimoga district, Karnataka, India, from virgin LLDPE resin.
Why is there no price on the website?
Film prices depend on width, thickness, roll length, quantity and delivery location, so we quote every order. Send your requirement on the form or on WhatsApp and we will reply with a price.
