Questions

Asked by buyers like you.

Which thickness of stretch film should I buy?
For hand wrapping light to medium pallets, 15–20 µm is usually enough. For loads with sharp edges, use 20–25 µm. On an automatic wrapper, 17–23 µm suits regular pallets and 23–30 µm suits heavy or sharp-edged loads. Send us your load details and we will confirm.
What sizes of stretch film do you make?
Hand grade in 300, 350, 450 and 500 mm widths and machine grade in 500 and 600 mm, from 12 to 35 µm thick. Roll lengths run from 200 m to 3,600 m depending on width.
Do you give a GST invoice?
Yes. Every order in India is billed with a proper GST invoice.
Where do you deliver?
We deliver quickly across Karnataka and South India from our factory in Shimoga, and dispatch to the rest of India too.
Is your stretch film made from recycled plastic?
No. It is made from virgin LLDPE resin, with no reprocessed blends.
Why is there no price on the website?
Film prices depend on width, thickness, roll length, quantity and delivery location, so we quote every order. Send your requirement on the form or on WhatsApp and we will reply with a price.
