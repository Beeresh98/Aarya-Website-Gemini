Questions

Asked by buyers like you.

How long does REINLAND last in the sun?
REINLAND-60 is UV-stabilised for 10–12 months and REINLAND-400 for 18 months, so wrapped bales can stand outdoors through the season.
Which REINLAND should I choose?
REINLAND-60 is the everyday, cost-effective wrap. Choose REINLAND-400 if bales will be stored for a long time or stand in strong sun.
What sizes and colours are available?
Widths of 250, 350, 500 and 750 mm with 1,500–1,800 m on each roll. Colours are green, light green, white and black, plus white and transparent in the wider REINLAND-60 rolls.
Is REINLAND made in India?
Yes. REINLAND is made by Aarya Plastopet at the Machenahalli Production Unit in Shimoga, Karnataka.
Do you supply dealers?
Yes. We supply dealers and large dairy farms directly from the factory. Send your details and monthly volume.
Why is there no price on the website?
Film prices depend on width, thickness, roll length, quantity and delivery location, so we quote every order. Send your requirement on the form or on WhatsApp and we will reply with a price.
